Customization

A Fotobogen site is yours: your name, your words, your look, at your address. This page lists everything that can be changed, where it is, and how.

Where things are changed

There are three places, and each kind of setting lives in exactly one of them.

PlaceWhat is set thereWho can change itTakes effect
The admin, at /adminEverything about the site: its name, words, look, photos, and what search engines are toldYou, logged in, from any browserAt once, on the next page view
Folders in dataSkins and modules you addWhoever can put files on the serverAfter a restart
.env on the serverHow the app runs: port, address, data folder, upload limitsWhoever can log in to the serverAfter a restart

Nothing in the admin needs a rebuild, a restart, or any knowledge of code. What is set in the admin is stored in the site's database, so it is included in every backup and survives updates.

Its name and its words

Where: Admin, Appearance, Site text.

Nothing a visitor sees has to say "Fotobogen". The software's name appears only in the admin.

WhatHowNotes
Site titleThe Site title fieldAt the top of every page, in the browser tab, in search results, and in the feed. Up to 80 characters.
TaglineThe Tagline fieldA short line beside the title. Optional. Also the site's description unless you write one.
LanguageThe Language field: a code such as en, de, pt-BRTells screen readers how to pronounce the site and search engines what language it is in. It does not translate anything.

The groups of text are: header and footer; the photo grid; a photo; albums and tags; the slideshow; and what is shown when a page cannot be. To run the site in another language, set the language code and reword the fields. A few pieces of text contain names in curly brackets, such as {count}; keep those, as they are filled in when the page is shown.

What cannot be changed here: your photos' own titles and descriptions (those are on each photo's page in the Library), album names (Albums), and the licence wording (Settings). Text is always shown as text: HTML typed into a field appears as typed and is not run.

Its icon and logo

Where: Admin, Appearance, Icon and logo. Both are optional. Without them the site shows its name as text, and the browser tab has no icon.

WhatHowNotes
IconChoose an icon, and pick a pictureShown in the browser tab, in bookmarks, and on a phone's home screen. Use a square picture of 512 by 512 pixels; at least 64. One that is not square is cropped to its middle. A simple shape reads better than a photograph at this size.
LogoChoose a logo, for light mode, dark mode, or bothShown at the top of every page in place of the site's name. The name stays as its description for screen readers and search engines. A PNG with a transparent background works best.

What is accepted: PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 5 MB, judged by what is inside the file. SVG is refused, because an SVG file can carry scripts; export it as a PNG. The picture is redrawn before it is kept, so only its pixels are stored. Both pictures are kept in the site's database, so they are in every backup.

Counting visits

Fotobogen counts nothing itself, and until you choose a service your pages load nothing from anywhere else. Admin, Visitor statistics adds a counting service of your choice to the public pages:

ServiceWhat you give
PlausibleThe script address from the snippet Plausible shows you (the part inside src="…"). For the older snippet, also its data-domain value.
Google AnalyticsYour measurement ID, such as G-AB12CD34EF.
Another serviceIts script address, any data- attributes from its script tag, and, rarely, a second address it reports to. This covers Umami, Fathom, GoatCounter, Simple Analytics, Cloudflare Web Analytics, and others installed with one script tag.

You give an address or an ID, and Fotobogen writes the script tag. No code is typed into the admin, and nothing typed there is ever run as script.

  • Public pages only. The script is never on the admin, the login, or setup, and never while you are logged in, so your own visits are not counted. To check it works, open your site in a private window.
  • One service, and nothing else. Your pages may report to the service you chose. Everything else stays blocked.
  • Only you can change it, logged in. No API token can, and a demo visitor cannot.
  • You are trusting the service, with your visitors and with your site. Its script runs on every visitor's page, on your site's own address. Fotobogen keeps it off the admin and off pages you open while logged in, but it cannot fence a script off from the site it runs on: if the service were ever hostile or broken into, a public page left open in a browser where you are logged in could be used to act as you. Choose a service you would trust that far, or close the gap altogether by giving the admin its own address.
  • What you owe your visitors is yours to decide. Whether you owe visitors a privacy note or a cookie banner is yours to decide; Google Analytics generally needs one in the EU and UK, and cookieless services generally do not. Fotobogen shows no banner.
  • No box for pasting script. JavaScript of your own cannot be added from the admin, on purpose: such a box would let anyone who got your password put code in every visitor's browser.

Showing your photos on another site

Other sites can show a slideshow of your photos inside their own pages: a blog, a club's site, a portfolio. Open Admin, Embed on another site, switch it on, choose every photo, one album, or one tag, and a size, and copy the one line of code the page gives you:

<iframe src="https://photos.example.com/embed/slideshow" width="800" height="600" style="border:0;max-width:100%;height:auto;aspect-ratio:800/600" title="Slideshow: Kodachrome Days" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Paste it where the other site lets you add HTML: an "HTML", "Embed", or "Code" block in most editors. The slideshow has one bar of controls (first, back, play or pause, forward, last) with "Powered by Fotobogen" at the end, linked to fotobogen.com. The arrow keys, Home, End, and Space work too. The width and height are the largest it will be: on a narrower page or a phone it shrinks to fit and keeps its shape.

  • Off until you switch it on. While it is off, its address answers "not found". Switching it off again stops it at once on every site that has the code.
  • Any site can show it, once it is on, not only yours. It plays the photos anyone can already see on your site and nothing else: never a hidden, draft, or scheduled photo, and never where a photo was taken.
  • Only this slideshow can be shown inside another site. Every other page still refuses to be framed by anyone else, and the admin and the login refuse even your own site.
  • Pressing a photo opens its page on your site, in a new tab.
  • It plays as your own slideshow does: the seconds per photo and the order are the ones under Admin, Settings. It has its own plain dark look, not your skin, because it sits in a page that is not yours.
  • Visits to it are not counted. It loads no counting script, whatever is set under Visitor statistics.
  • Only you can switch it, logged in. No API token can, and a demo visitor cannot.
  • Switching it off does not hide your photos. They stay public on your site, and the public API is still readable from any site, so someone who writes their own code can show them.
  • It plays from your server. Each person who sees it on another site fetches it from here, so a busy site that embeds it adds to your server's work, and your server's log shows which pages embed it.
  • Some hosted site builders remove frames from pasted code. There the slideshow cannot be shown; link to its address instead.

Letting other programs upload

Where: Admin, Server, API tokens.

A token lets a script, or a program such as a photo editor's export plug-in, add photos to your site without your password. Make one, give it a name, and choose what it may do:

KindMayMay not
Upload onlyUpload photos, list the library, see whether a photo has been processedChange or delete anything
Manage photosAll of that, and edit, reorder, hide, and delete photos, set their looks, and manage albumsChange anything about the site itself

No token can change settings, skins, site text, visitor statistics, or embedding, make or revoke tokens, download a backup, or restore one. The token is shown once, when it is made; copy it then. Revoking a token stops it at once. How to use one.

Its address

The site answers at whatever address reaches the server. To use your own domain:

  1. Point the domain at the server (an A record with whoever you bought the domain from).
  2. Give the server a certificate for it. With the Docker quick start, set DOMAIN in .env and Caddy does it; on Forge, issue it in Forge.
  3. Tell Fotobogen the address: Search and sharing, Your site's proper address, or SITE_URL in .env. Links copied from the admin, the feed, and the sitemap then use it.

What cannot be customized

  • The admin's own appearance. It is dark and says "Fotobogen". Only you see it.
  • The structure of the public pages. A skin restyles them thoroughly but cannot rearrange or add to them. There are no custom pages (an "About" page, say) yet.
  • The menu at the top of the site. Its links can be reworded, but not removed, reordered, or added to.
  • The sizes of the resized copies. They are fixed; see why.
  • More than one account. There is one admin.

Several of these are planned. The roadmap says which.
