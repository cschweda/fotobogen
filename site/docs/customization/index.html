Customization

A Fotobogen site is yours: your name, your words, your look, at your address. This page lists everything that can be changed, where it is, and how.

Where things are changed

There are three places, and each kind of setting lives in exactly one of them.

PlaceWhat is set thereWho can change itTakes effect
The admin, at /adminEverything about the site: its name, words, look, photos, and what search engines are toldYou, logged in, from any browserAt once, on the next page view
Folders in dataSkins and modules you addWhoever can put files on the serverAfter a restart
.env on the serverHow the app runs: port, address, data folder, upload limitsWhoever can log in to the serverAfter a restart

Nothing in the admin needs a rebuild, a restart, or any knowledge of code. What is set in the admin is stored in the site's database, so it is included in every backup and survives updates.

Its name and its words

Where: Admin, Appearance, Site text.

Nothing a visitor sees has to say "Fotobogen". The software's name appears only in the admin.

WhatHowNotes
Site titleThe Site title fieldAt the top of every page, in the browser tab, in search results, and in the feed. Up to 80 characters.
TaglineThe Tagline fieldA short line beside the title. Optional. Also the site's description unless you write one.
LanguageThe Language field: a code such as en, de, pt-BRTells screen readers how to pronounce the site and search engines what language it is in. It does not translate anything.
Every label, link, button, and messageThe fields under Words on the site, grouped by where they appearAbout fifty pieces of text. Each field shows the wording used now; type your own to replace it. Up to 200 characters each.
A footer line of your ownA line of your own in the footer, under Header and footerEmpty to begin with. A copyright notice, for instance.
Back to the original wordingEmpty a field, or press Use the default wording everywhere

The groups of text are: header and footer; the photo grid; a photo; albums and tags; the slideshow; and what is shown when a page cannot be. To run the site in another language, set the language code and reword the fields. A few pieces of text contain names in curly brackets, such as {count}; keep those, as they are filled in when the page is shown.

What cannot be changed here: your photos' own titles and descriptions (those are on each photo's page in the Library), album names (Albums), and the licence wording (Settings). Text is always shown as text: HTML typed into a field appears as typed and is not run.

Its icon and logo

Where: Admin, Appearance, Icon and logo. Both are optional. Without them the site shows its name as text, and the browser tab has no icon.

WhatHowNotes
IconChoose an icon, and pick a pictureShown in the browser tab, in bookmarks, and on a phone's home screen. Use a square picture of 512 by 512 pixels; at least 64. One that is not square is cropped to its middle. A simple shape reads better than a photograph at this size.
LogoChoose a logo, for light mode, dark mode, or bothShown at the top of every page in place of the site's name. The name stays as its description for screen readers and search engines. A PNG with a transparent background works best.
A different logo for dark modeUpload a second one under For dark modeA logo that reads well on a light page may vanish on a dark one. With only one, it is used for both.
How tall the logo isThe slider, then Save sizeFrom 20 to 120 pixels; 40 to begin with. A logo is never drawn larger than the picture you gave.
Going backRemove beside eachThe name returns as text.

What is accepted: PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 5 MB, judged by what is inside the file. SVG is refused, because an SVG file can carry scripts; export it as a PNG. The picture is redrawn before it is kept, so only its pixels are stored. Both pictures are kept in the site's database, so they are in every backup.

Letting other programs upload

Where: Admin, Server, API tokens.

A token lets a script, or a program such as a photo editor's export plug-in, add photos to your site without your password. Make one, give it a name, and choose what it may do:

KindMayMay not
Upload onlyUpload photos, list the library, see whether a photo has been processedChange or delete anything
Manage photosAll of that, and edit, reorder, hide, and delete photos, set their looks, and manage albumsChange anything about the site itself

No token can change settings, skins, or site text, make or revoke tokens, download a backup, or restore one. The token is shown once, when it is made; copy it then. Revoking a token stops it at once. How to use one.

Its address

The site answers at whatever address reaches the server. To use your own domain:

  1. Point the domain at the server (an A record with whoever you bought the domain from).
  2. Give the server a certificate for it. With the Docker quick start, set DOMAIN in .env and Caddy does it; on Forge, issue it in Forge.
  3. Tell Fotobogen the address: Search and sharing, Your site's proper address, or SITE_URL in .env. Links copied from the admin, the feed, and the sitemap then use it.

What cannot be customized

  • The admin's own appearance. It is dark and says "Fotobogen". Only you see it.
  • The structure of the public pages. A skin restyles them thoroughly but cannot rearrange or add to them. There are no custom pages (an "About" page, say) yet.
  • The menu at the top of the site. Its links can be reworded, but not removed, reordered, or added to.
  • The sizes of the resized copies. They are fixed; see why.
  • More than one account. There is one admin.

Several of these are planned. The roadmap says which.
